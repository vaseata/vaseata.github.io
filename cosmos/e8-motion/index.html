About This Motion

What you're seeing

240 root vectors of the E8 lattice, rotating in 8-dimensional space and projected down to 3D. The rotation occurs on independent 2-planes — there is no single axis as in 3D.

Why it shimmers

In 8D you have 28 independent rotation planes (compared to just 3 in our familiar space). Several are running simultaneously here at incommensurate frequencies, so the configuration never quite repeats.

The Lattice

E8 is the unique even unimodular lattice in 8 dimensions. Its 240 roots are the smallest non-zero vectors. Each touches 240 others at minimum distance — the kissing number, proven optimal for 8D in 1979.
